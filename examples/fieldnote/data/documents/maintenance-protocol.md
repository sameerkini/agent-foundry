# Maintenance evidence and causal interpretation

## Fictional protocol
This protocol belongs to the fictional Fieldnote demonstration. Maintenance records are operational notes, not controlled experiments.

## What a record supports
A maintenance event supports that a described action or observation was recorded for that sensor on that date. It does not establish that the action caused a later change, solved the problem, improved measurement accuracy, or prevented a future failure. An inspection is not a calibration or a battery replacement. A temporal association is not causal evidence.

## Stronger evidence
To assess a repair, request an independent calibrated reference, documented before/after measurements under comparable conditions, instrument specifications, and a stated acceptance criterion. Preserve caveats when quoting a report. The SQL database supplies counts and measurements; field reports supply context and qualifications. A mixed answer should cite both when its conclusion uses both.

## Battery decisions
This corpus supplies no manufacturer-specific replacement threshold, capacity measurement, or remaining-life model. Voltage alone cannot establish remaining lifetime. A suspect low reading should trigger repeat measurement and review of the relevant instrument specification, rather than an invented replacement threshold or an unconditional replacement recommendation.
